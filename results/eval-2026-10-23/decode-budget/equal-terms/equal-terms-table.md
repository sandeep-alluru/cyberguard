## Why this table had to be built: the surface effect, measured

The base arm appears in the record on the **all-benchmarks surface** (4,110 items) and in this sweep on the **CTI-MCQ-only surface** (2,500 items). Same model, same greedy decoder, same parser, same CTI-MCQ items — differing only in what else shared the batches.

## The VSP ladder

CTI-VSP (1,000 items), the same runner and the same ladder. The question this answers is narrow: **is the base's CTI-VSP format collapse (998 of 1,000 unparseable at 512) an artefact of the budget, or of the model?**

**The metric is not accuracy.** CTI-VSP scores a CVSS v3.1 base-score prediction by **mean absolute deviation in CVSS points — lower is better** — and the reference scorer **skips** responses it cannot parse, so the denominator is the responses that parsed, not the population. A MAD over two parsed responses is not a capability measurement, and that is the point of this ladder.
