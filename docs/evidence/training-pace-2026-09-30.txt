# Training pace vs the time ceiling — the objective's biggest remaining risk

An INCOMPLETE epoch is a NO-SHIP (§5.1) and would waste the whole run, so "will it finish in budget?" is
the one question worth measuring repeatedly rather than eyeballing.

## MEASURED (trainer's own live progress line, step 1848/8783)

    step                    : 1848 / 8783  (21.0%)
    elapsed                 : 7.78 h
    measured rate           : 15.15 s/step
    projected total         : 133,047 s = 36.96 h = 2,217 min
    trainer's own remaining : 30.65 h        (consistent)
    ceiling (max_minutes)   : 3,300 min = 55.00 h
    margin                  : 1,083 min = 18.0 h

**VERDICT: comfortably inside the ceiling.** No intervention indicated; GPU at 93 %.

## WHAT THIS SETTLES ABOUT THE BUDGET CHANGE

The plan as approved carried `max_minutes = 1320` (22 h), derived from v2's presentations/second. At the
measured rate that cap would have stopped the run at

    step 5,228 / 8,783  =  60 % of the epoch

which §5.1 disposes as INCOMPLETE — adapter not harvested, no ship verdict. So:

* **The raise was NECESSARY**: 1,320 min < 2,217 min projected; the original cap could not have finished
  the epoch at any plausible rate.
* **The raise was SUFFICIENT**: 3,300 min > 2,217 min with 18 h of margin.
* It changed no hyperparameter — `max_minutes` is a TimeBox ceiling, and the run still ends when epoch
  1.0 completes.

The earlier token-density projection was ~45 h; the measured pace is ~37 h. The projection was
deliberately stated as an arithmetic estimate with its transfer assumption exposed rather than as a
demonstrated bound, and the measurement has now superseded it — in the conservative direction for the
budget decision (both figures exceed the old cap by a wide margin).

## METHOD NOTE

Computed from the trainer's own progress line, not from my own clock, so it is immune to how long any
particular supervision round took. The arithmetic is reproducible from the two numbers printed above.
