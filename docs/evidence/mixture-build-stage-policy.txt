## Reading

- The refusal result (1,200 -> 1,198) **exactly reproduces** v2's pinned FACTS.json
  (`refusal_unique_rows = 1198`, 700 offensive-refusal + 498 unknown-answer), so the v2 mechanism IS
  applied to the authored sets and the plan's 9,584 slots / 140,545 total are reproducible.
- The merged result is the reason the corpora must NOT be re-deduped. v2's dedupe collapses on the
  PROMPT, and merged's prompts share a CVE-analysis template while the CVEs underneath differ
  (PREP_SUMMARY.md records the same for primus). Collapsing them would delete two-thirds of a source
  and move the epoch by ~15,000 presentations — a silent change to the training mixture.

## Policy adopted (written into PLAN-V3 §2 pre-flight)

- Three public corpora: enter the mixture **exactly as prepped** — no near-dup collapse, no
  near-holdout removal, no boundary/defect dropping, no answer clipping; role-mapping only
  (`messages` -> flat `{system?,user,assistant}`). Counts are the SFT_MANIFEST.md counts.
- Authored sets: through the v2 mechanism (refusal -> 1,198, format -> 1,300). Replay capped at 594.
- Answer clipping is not applied at build time (it changes content without changing counts); §5.3
  measures real truncation at max_seq 2048.
- Any deviation is a build failure, and the receipt reports realised per-source counts.
