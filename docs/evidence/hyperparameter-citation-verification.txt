## §5.2 stop rule is defined on a series that exists

The rule is "median of the first 30 logged observations after warmup; halt if the median of the
trailing 30 exceeds 2× baseline for 6 consecutive observations". The receipt's `log_history` contains
**416 entries, 415 with a loss**, and the **step delta is exactly 10** throughout (10, 20, 30, …,
4158) — so "every 10 optimizer steps" is real, and 415 observations comfortably support a 30-observation
baseline plus a 6-consecutive halt condition. `grad_norm` is logged on all 415. The rule is
reproducible from the receipt, which is what review pass-1 finding 9 demanded.
