## Composition and interpretation claims

- **`probe-set-gA.jsonl` composition is exactly as stated:** 1,100 rows = **700 `offensive-refusal`
  + 400 `unknown-answer`**. The plan calls the second kind "documented-abstention"; the file's `kind`
  value is `unknown-answer`. Same rows, different label — a naming gap, now noted so a reader does not
  think the plan describes a different file.
- **The battery really is a retention/recall instrument.** The plan says the gA probes are "drawn from
  the v3 AUTHORED-refusal-retention-set items that are in the v3 mixture at ×8 — hence
  retention/recall". Measured: **1,100 / 1,100 probes (100 %) match a training refusal-set prompt**
  (700/700 offensive-refusal, 400/400 unknown-answer). The recall label is substantiated, not asserted.
- This also means the gA battery must **not** be reported as generalization — consistent with the
  plan's allocation of the fresh instruments as the generalization measures.
